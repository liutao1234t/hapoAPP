Privacy Policy

This Policy describes how Omer handles information when you browse fashion posts, join outfit challenges, upload looks, or use our AI styling tools.

01 / Overview

Who this Policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the Omer mobile application and related services (the “Service”). “Omer,” “we,” “us” and “our” refer to the operator of the Service. This Policy should be read with our Terms of Service.

We aim to collect and process only information reasonably necessary to operate the features you use. We do not intentionally collect information unrelated to those purposes, and we do not sell, rent, trade or disclose personal information to unrelated third parties.

Information that may be processed

The information involved depends on which functions you choose to use. The screenshots and feature design show the following relevant categories:

If a future feature requires a new category of information, we will update this Policy and provide notices or choices required by law.

03 / Photos and submissions

How we handle photos and user content

When you choose to upload an outfit photo or submit content to a challenge, we process that material to receive, store, display and administer the submission. When you provide an image or text to an AI styling feature, we process it to generate the result you requested.

Please submit only images and information you have the right to use. Avoid including sensitive documents or information that is not needed for styling. You should consider that a post submitted to a community feed or challenge may be visible to other users according to the feature’s presentation and settings.

We do not use photos for purposes unrelated to providing and protecting the Service. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect photos, prompts and other personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration.

04 / AI processing

AI Outfit Generator and AI Style Assistant

Omer’s AI models are self-developed, self-trained and self-deployed by us. We do not use third-party AI model services to process your prompts or generate styling results. Information you submit to an AI feature is processed for the purpose of providing that feature, such as producing an outfit suggestion or answering a styling question.

We do not intentionally use AI inputs for unrelated purposes. AI results may be imperfect and are not guaranteed to be accurate or suitable. Do not include information about yourself or another person that is unnecessary to your request.

05 / Use and disclosure

Why we use information

We use information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Operate the fashion feed, follows, likes, saved posts and sharing functions;
  • Receive and display outfit challenge submissions;
  • Generate AI styling suggestions and respond to styling questions;
  • Maintain account and purchase entitlements where those functions are offered;
  • Respond to support requests, protect the Service, prevent abuse and enforce our Terms; and
  • Comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We do not share it with unrelated third parties. We may disclose the minimum information reasonably necessary to a service provider acting on our behalf for an essential operational task, where such provider is subject to appropriate confidentiality and use restrictions. This does not include third-party AI model services.

Where a purchase is made through an app store, the platform processes payment and may provide us with limited transaction or entitlement information so the purchase can be recognized. The platform’s own privacy policy and terms also apply. If you choose to share a post through an external sharing function, the destination service receives information according to your action and that service’s policies.

06 / Retention

How long information is kept

We retain information for no longer than reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including providing the Service, maintaining a submission or account you choose to keep, handling support requests, protecting the Service and meeting legal obligations.

When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete it or render it no longer associated with you. Limited copies may remain temporarily in routine backups or be retained where necessary for security, legal compliance, fraud prevention or dispute resolution.

07 / Your choices and rights

Your privacy choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of or a copy of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also be able to withdraw a permission you previously granted, subject to the effect this has on the related feature.

  • Content: Use available in-app controls to manage or remove content you submitted, where provided.
  • Photos: You control whether to select and submit a photo. You can choose not to use photo-based features.
  • Account: If account controls are available, use them to manage your account. You may contact us for assistance.
  • Purchases: Manage or cancel app-store subscriptions through the relevant app-store account. Purchase refunds are subject to the platform’s process and applicable law.

To make a privacy request, email support@omer.store. Tell us what you are requesting and enough information to help us locate the relevant data. We may need to verify your request before acting. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

08 / Security

How we protect information

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect user photos, submitted content, prompts and other personal information. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should protect your device and account credentials and avoid submitting information that is not needed for the feature.

09 / Children

Children’s privacy

Omer is not intended for children under 13, or any higher minimum age required by local law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at support@omer.store so we can review the request and take appropriate steps.

10 / International use and changes

International processing and updates

Your information may be processed in the country or region where we or essential service providers operate. Where cross-border transfer rules apply, we will take steps required by applicable law to protect the information.

We may update this Policy when the Service or applicable law changes. We will publish the revised version here and update the effective date. If a change materially affects your privacy rights, we will provide additional notice where required.
