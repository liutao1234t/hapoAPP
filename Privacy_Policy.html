Privacy Policy

What Hapo handles, why it is needed, how long it stays, and the choices you have—without burying the important parts.

Our AI is ours. Your information is yours.

Hapo’s AI models are independently developed, trained, deployed, and operated by us. We do not use third-party AI model services to provide Hapo’s AI features.

We do not collect information unrelated to Hapo’s functions. We do not sell, trade, rent, or share user information. We strictly protect user privacy with limited access, technical safeguards, and purpose-bound handling.

Our privacy baseline

We design Hapo around data minimization. We handle information only when it is connected to a feature you use, security, support, or a legal requirement. We do not collect information unrelated to Hapo’s functions.

Information you provide

  • Account details, such as a username, profile image, and contact details you choose to provide.
  • Photos submitted for AI try-on, posts, captions, messages, follows, saves, reactions, and reports.
  • Support communications and information needed to answer your request.
  • Purchase status or credit balance. Payment-card details are handled by the relevant app store or payment provider, not by Hapo.

Information created through use

We may process limited technical and activity information needed to operate Hapo, such as device and app version, session timing, feature actions, diagnostics, security events, and approximate network information.

We do not seek precise location, contacts, health data, or unrelated device content unless a future feature clearly requires it and you give any permission required by law.

How we use information

  • Provide accounts, feeds, follows, saves, messages, reports, and support.
  • Generate the AI try-on you request and return the result.
  • Maintain credits, prevent fraud and abuse, diagnose failures, and protect the service.
  • Comply with binding legal obligations and enforce our rules.
  • Improve Hapo using limited, aggregated, or de-identified operational insights where feasible.

Our self-operated AI

Developed by us · trained by us · deployed by us · operated by us

Hapo’s AI models are independently developed, trained, deployed, and operated on infrastructure under our control. We do not send your try-on photo or prompt to a third-party AI model service.

Your submitted photo is used to provide the requested try-on and maintain service safety. We do not use private photos or private messages to train our AI models without separate, explicit consent.

No sale or sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share user information. We do not disclose user information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We may use infrastructure vendors acting only as tightly controlled processors for hosting, storage, delivery, security, or support. They may process information solely under our instructions, must protect it, and may not use it for their own purposes. This operational processing is not a sale or independent sharing of your information.

Permissions

Camera or photo-library access is requested only when you choose to capture or select an image. Notification access is requested only to deliver alerts you enable. You can change permissions in device settings, although some features may then be unavailable.

Retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as needed for the feature, account, security, dispute, or legal purpose involved. AI input photos and generated results follow short, purpose-limited retention unless you intentionally save or publish the result.

When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify associated information within a reasonable period, except limited records required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or unresolved disputes. Backup copies expire through normal rotation.

Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, role-based permissions, monitoring, secure development practices, and retention limits. No system can promise absolute security, but we strictly protect user privacy and continuously review safeguards.

If an incident creates a legally reportable risk, we will notify affected users and authorities as required.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection, and may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also appeal a denied request where local law provides.

Email Support from an address connected to your account. We may verify your identity before fulfilling a request and will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

Children

Hapo is not directed to children below the minimum digital-consent age in their region. We do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has provided information without valid consent, contact Support for prompt review and deletion.

International use and updates

Hapo may be accessed across regions. Where information is processed outside your location, we apply protections required by applicable law.

We may update this Policy as Hapo evolves. Material changes will receive reasonable notice, and the effective date will be updated.
